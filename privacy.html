Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what Prodigal$ Blaster, developed by Prodigal$ Brothers, collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile photo link and account ID, provided through Google.
  • Cloud save: your account ID, display name, coins, items, skins, powers, settings, best score and the time of the last update.
  • Leaderboard: your account ID, display name, score and time of update. Display names and scores are visible to other players.
  • Guest play: an anonymous account ID, a random name such as "Anonymous 1234", and your score if it is sent to the leaderboard. Game progress is kept temporarily in your browser or app.
  • On your device: browser storage keeps progress, settings and sign-in state.
  • Technical data: Google's services may process data such as IP address and device or browser information to run and protect sign-in and the database.

The Game does not show ads and does not sell your personal information.

How we use it

To sign you in, save and restore progress, show the leaderboard, prevent abuse and fix problems.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Data may be processed on servers outside your country. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account data until you delete your account or ask us to. You can delete it in the Game or on the Delete Account page. Deletion removes your cloud save, leaderboard entry and sign-in account for the Game. Copies in Google's backup systems are removed according to Google's schedules.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, including rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. Contact us at the address below. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child gave us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and changes

We use Google's sign-in and database access rules to protect data, but no system is completely secure. We may update this policy and will change the date above.
